Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how meowfinglobal.com ("we," "us," or "our") handles user information. We respect and are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Do Not Collect

We make the following solemn commitment to you:

  • No PII Collection: This website does not actively collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, IP addresses, etc.).
  • Local Data Processing: All data you enter into the financial tools on this website (e.g., financial figures, options data, investment parameters) is processed locally within your browser. This data is never sent to our servers or any third parties. All calculations are performed directly on your device.

Technologies and Third-Party Services

Local Storage (localStorage)

To enhance your user experience, we use the browser's `localStorage` feature to save your language preference settings. This information is stored solely in your own browser; we cannot access it. It ensures that you see your selected language interface upon your next visit.

Third-Party Service: Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service, to display ads. This is how we maintain the operation of the website.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt-out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad Settings page to disable personalized advertising.
  • We do not have access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers. We suggest you review their respective privacy policies for more detailed information.

External Links

This website may contain links to other websites (such as our YouTube channel). Please note that we are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy statements of every website you visit when you leave our site.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Any changes are effective immediately upon posting on this page.
